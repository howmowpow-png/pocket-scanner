# Pocket Scanner

Scan paper documents with your phone camera and save them as a PDF — all in the browser, nothing uploaded.

**Open it:** https://howmowpow-png.github.io/pocket-scanner/

- Snap pages or pick photos, drag the corners to straighten each page
- Looks: Original, Enhanced (removes shadows), Grayscale, B&W
- Reorder or delete pages, choose Fit / Letter / A4, then Save PDF (uses the share sheet on phones)

On iPhone: open the link in Safari → Share → **Add to Home Screen** for an app icon.
